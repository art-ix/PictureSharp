libjpeg-turbo (https://libjpeg-turbo.org), version 2.1.5, based on the
Independent JPEG Group's libjpeg
Used for: JPEG decoder (marker parsing and restart resynchronization from
jdmarker.c, Huffman decoding from jdhuff.c/jdphuff.c, accurate integer inverse
DCT from jidctint.c, fancy upsampling from jdsample.c, color conversion from
jdcolor.c), and JPEG encoder (quality tables and progressive scan script from
jcparam.c, RGB conversion from jccolor.c, downsampling from jcsample.c,
accurate integer forward DCT from jfdctint.c, quantization from jcdctmgr.c,
coefficient padding from jccoefct.c, Huffman encoding and optimal tables from
jchuff.c/jcphuff.c, marker writing from jcmarker.c). The code was translated
to C# and restructured; it is not the original source.
